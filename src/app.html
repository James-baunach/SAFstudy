Trees and stands

Basal area of a tree (sq ft)
BA = 0.005454 × D²
Quadratic mean diameter
QMD = √(BA per acre ÷ (0.005454 × TPA))
Reineke stand density index
SDI = TPA × (QMD ÷ 10)^1.605
Trees per acre at a spacing
TPA = 43,560 ÷ (row spacing × tree spacing)
Mean and periodic annual increment
MAI = volume ÷ age · PAI = (V₂ − V₁) ÷ years between
q-ratio (uneven-aged)
q = trees in a class ÷ trees in the next larger class
Genetic gain
R = h² × S

Plots and prisms

Plot radius
r = √(plot area in sq ft ÷ π) · 1/100 ac 11.8 ft · 1/20 ac 26.3 ft · 1/10 ac 37.2 ft · 1/5 ac 52.7 ft
Fixed-plot expansion factor
EF = 1 ÷ plot size in acres
Prism basal area per acre
BA = 'in' count × BAF
Trees per acre per tally tree
TF = BAF ÷ (0.005454 × D²)
Plot radius factor (ft per inch of DBH)
PRF = 8.696 ÷ √BAF · BAF 10 = 2.75 · BAF 20 = 1.94
Limiting distance
LD = PRF × D
Volume from VBAR
Volume per acre = BA per acre × VBAR

Sampling statistics

Coefficient of variation
CV% = s ÷ mean × 100
Standard error of the mean
SE = s ÷ √n
Confidence interval
mean ± t × SE (t ≈ 2 for 95%)
Sampling error (percent)
E% = t × SE ÷ mean × 100
Sample size
n = t² × CV² ÷ E²

Heights, slopes, and logs

Height, percent clinometer
H = horizontal distance × (top% − base%) ÷ 100 (base reading is negative below eye level)
Height, degree clinometer
H = horizontal distance × (tan top − tan base)
Percent slope
% = rise ÷ run × 100 · 45° = 100%
Slope correction
HD = SD × cos(arctan(% ÷ 100)) · SD = HD ÷ cos(…)
Smalian's formula
V = (B + b) ÷ 2 × L
Huber's formula
V = M × L
Newton's formula
V = (B + 4M + b) ÷ 6 × L
Doyle log rule
bd ft = (D − 4)² × L ÷ 16 (D = small-end DIB)
Overrun
% = (lumber tally − log scale) ÷ log scale × 100

NDVI

Traverse precision

Money and time

Future and present value
FV = PV × (1 + i)ⁿ · PV = FV ÷ (1 + i)ⁿ
Terminating annual series
PV = a × [(1 + i)ⁿ − 1] ÷ [i × (1 + i)ⁿ] · FV = a × [(1 + i)ⁿ − 1] ÷ i
Perpetual annual series
PV = a ÷ i
Perpetual periodic series (every t years)
PV = a ÷ [(1 + i)ᵗ − 1]
Land expectation value (Faustmann)
LEV = NPV of one rotation × (1 + i)^R ÷ [(1 + i)^R − 1]
IRR of a single payment
IRR = (FV ÷ PV)^(1/n) − 1
Real interest rate
(1 + nominal) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1 ≈ nominal − inflation
Rule of 72
years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rate in percent
Benefit/cost ratio
PV of benefits ÷ PV of costs
Residual stumpage
delivered price − logging − hauling − profit and risk
Depletion
unit = adjusted basis ÷ volume · deduction = unit × volume sold
Area and volume control
acres per year = area ÷ rotation · Hanzlik AC = Vm ÷ R + I
